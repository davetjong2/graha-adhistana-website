About

Since 2002, the foundation first.

We design and build private residences in Pantai Indah Kapuk and across Jakarta. One studio carries each house from first sketch to final handover.

01

The name

Graha
iHouse
Adhiṣṭhāna
iiPlinth

Graha means house. In classical Indian temple architecture, the adhiṣṭhāna is the plinth, the base on which the whole temple rises.

So we begin where the temple begins, with the foundation. Structure, proportion and craft come first; everything else rests on them.

02 Philosophy

What stays constant

  1. 01

    One studio, one line of responsibility

    Design and construction stay with one team. The people who draw your house are the people who build it.

  2. 02

    Materials, used honestly

    Stone, timber and light, each allowed to be itself. We choose few materials and use them well.

  3. 03

    Built to endure

    We draw each house for the decades after handover. Proportion and structure outlast fashion.
