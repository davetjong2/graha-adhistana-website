Styles

Three languages, one standard

Modern tropical or neoclassical, a whole house or a single room. The language follows the client and the site; the standard does not change.

Modern

Deep overhangs, timber and stone, sharp lines meeting fluid curves.

Classic

Neoclassical proportion, arched windows and French mansard roofs.

Interior

Dark, refined rooms in marble, walnut and soft light.

Questions

Before we begin

How does a project begin?

With a complimentary consultation. Message us on WhatsApp at +62 888 1010 688 and tell us about your site and what you have in mind.

Where do you work?

Our studio is in Pantai Indah Kapuk. We build across PIK, PIK 2 and Golf Island, including Ebony Golf, Sonata Lagoon and Katamaran, and elsewhere in Jakarta.

Will you build another architect’s design?

Yes. We work as general contractor for other architects. Future House in Jakarta, designed by Angkasa Architects, is one example.

Do you design interiors?

Yes. Our interiors range from marble living rooms and kitchens to home cinemas, bars and karaoke rooms.

Which styles do you work in?

Modern and modern tropical, contemporary, classic and neoclassical, and dark, refined interiors. Each commission takes the language that suits its owner.
